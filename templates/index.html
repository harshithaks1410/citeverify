Why CiteVerify is Different

🛡️

Anti-Fabrication Rule

Unlike standard LLM summaries, CiteVerify verifies every cited sentence against the actual paper abstract in code. Hallucinated or paraphrased quotes are instantly rejected.

⚖️

Quality-Weighted Consensus

We don't just count votes. Meta-analyses and large randomized controlled trials carry up to 3.5x the weight of observational reviews, scaled by log citations and recency.

🚫

Active Retraction Detection

Every paper DOI is queried against Crossref updates and PubMed tags. Retracted papers are highlighted in red and completely purged from consensus scores.

🎯

Honest Verdicts

When fewer than 3 papers exist or evidence is thin, CiteVerify raises an explicit weak-evidence warning and caps confidence. We never claim certainty on flimsy data.
